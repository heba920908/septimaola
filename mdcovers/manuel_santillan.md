## Performance Notes
- **Style:** Ska/reggae rhythm with emphasis on off-beats (typical ska upstroke pattern). The song has two distinct arrangements: a reggae version and a salsa/tumbao version
- **Bass lines:** The bass line is highly melodic and central to the song's drive, following the Gm root closely. In the salsa version, it features a tumbao feel with strong rhythmic interaction between bass and percussion. The bass emphasizes root and fifth patterns (Gm-D, Cm-G, D7-A) with walking bass elements during verses
- **Special arrangements:** Standard ska/reggae arrangement with potential for horn section accents. The song can be performed in both reggae and salsa styles
- **Instrumental breaks:** Intro and outro sections can be extended for solos or rhythmic variations
- **Key changes:** None - stays in Gm throughout
- **Dynamics:** Build energy through verses to chorus, maintain intensity in bridge. The bridge section ("Lo dijo el León") provides a dynamic contrast with the Eb-F-Gm progression
- **Rhythm pattern:** Characteristic ska/reggae rhythm with emphasis on beats 2 and 4, using barre chords to achieve the sharp, percussive "chack" sound common in ska
