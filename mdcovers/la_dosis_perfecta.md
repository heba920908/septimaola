_By Panteón Rococó_  -- status: revised

## Quick Chord Reference
**Key:** Cm (C minor / Do menor)

## Performance Notes
- **Style:** Ska rhythm with emphasis on off-beats (typical ska upstroke pattern)
- **Special arrangements:** Standard ska arrangement with horn section potential
- **Instrumental breaks:** Bridge section can be extended for solos
- **Key changes:** None - stays in Cm throughout
- **Dynamics:** Build energy through verses to chorus, maintain intensity in bridge
- **Rhythm pattern:** Characteristic ska rhythm with emphasis on beats 2 and 4
