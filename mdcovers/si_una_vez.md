## Performance Notes
- **Style:** Ska rhythm adaptation of the original cumbia/tejano style. Emphasis on off-beats (typical ska upstroke pattern on beats 2 and 4)
- **Bass lines:** Walking bass lines characteristic of ska, emphasizing the root notes on downbeats and moving through chord tones on upbeats. The bass should maintain the rhythmic drive while outlining the chord progression
- **Special arrangements:** Standard ska arrangement with horn section potential. The original cumbia rhythm is replaced with ska's characteristic off-beat guitar/piano stabs
- **Instrumental breaks:** Bridge section can be extended for solos, maintaining the ska groove
- **Key changes:** None - stays in Em throughout
- **Dynamics:** Build energy through verses to chorus, maintain intensity in bridge. The ska version should have more dynamic contrast than the original
- **Rhythm pattern:** Characteristic ska rhythm with emphasis on beats 2 and 4. Guitar/piano plays staccato chords on the off-beats while bass and drums maintain the driving rhythm
