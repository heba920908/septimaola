## Performance Notes
- **Style:** Reggae ballad groove with relaxed pocket and clean, sustained chord stabs.
- **Bass lines:** The bass works best with a warm, round tone and a root-fifth approach (G-D, D-A, Am-E, C-G). Add passing notes into chord changes, especially between D -> Am and Am -> C, to keep the line singing under the vocal.
- **Bass phrasing:** In verses, leave space on beat 1 and answer the guitar accents with short syncopated pickups. In pre-chorus and chorus, lock to steady eighth notes for more forward motion, then release into longer notes at phrase endings.
- **Special arrangements:** Keep rhythm guitar light in verses and let the bass carry melodic movement. Keys or guitar can double the C -> G transition in chorus for extra lift.
- **Instrumental breaks:** The intro progression can be looped for solos or for a vamp between vocal sections.
- **Key changes:** None - stays in G major throughout.
- **Dynamics:** Start intimate in the first verse, grow in pre-chorus, and open the low end in chorus with fuller bass sustain.
- **Rhythm pattern:** Classic reggae emphasis on off-beats from guitar/keys while bass anchors the downbeat and anticipates changes with short pickups.
