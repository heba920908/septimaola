## Performance Notes
- **Style:** Ska-rock with acoustic fingerstyle intro transitioning to clean ska rhythm, then to distorted power chord sections
- **Bass lines:** The bass follows the root notes of the chord progression, emphasizing the ska rhythm with emphasis on beats 2 and 4. During the acoustic intro, the bass provides a walking line that complements the fingerpicked guitar pattern. In the verse sections, the bass maintains a steady rhythm following the G-C9 progression, creating a solid foundation for the ska groove
- **Special arrangements:** Features prominent brass sections (horns) that are accompanied by specific guitar riffs. The song alternates between acoustic sections and distorted guitar sections, creating dynamic contrast
- **Instrumental breaks:** The brass/metal section provides space for horn solos and guitar riffs. The main guitar solo uses a driving rhythm on the lower strings (A and E strings), primarily moving between the 7th and 10th frets
- **Key changes:** None - stays in C throughout
- **Dynamics:** Begins with soft acoustic fingerstyle, builds energy through verses, reaches peak intensity in distorted chorus and brass sections
- **Rhythm pattern:** Characteristic ska rhythm with emphasis on off-beats (upstrokes on beats 2 and 4). The acoustic intro uses fingerpicking patterns, while the main sections use standard ska upstroke patterns
