## Performance Notes
- **Style:** Ska-punk rhythm with Latin influences, characteristic upstroke pattern on off-beats
- **Special arrangements:** Features dynamic horn section interplay, particularly in live versions
- **Instrumental breaks:** Bridge section allows for extended solos and instrumental showcases
- **Key changes:** None - maintains Am throughout the song
- **Dynamics:** Builds intensity from verses to chorus, with emotional peaks in the bridge section
- **Rhythm pattern:** Classic ska rhythm emphasizing beats 2 and 4, with syncopated accents
- **Live version:** Notable live performance features collaboration with María Barracuda, recorded during the 20th-anniversary concert (XX Años En Vivo, 2016)
