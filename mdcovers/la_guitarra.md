## Performance Notes
- **Style:** Ska-rock/punk rhythm with driving energy and characteristic ska upstroke patterns
- **Bass lines:** The bass follows a traditional ska and Argentine murga style, focusing on root and fifth of each chord (E-B, B-F#, A-E). During verses, it uses a walking bass feel, while the chorus features a more driving, eighth-note punk feel to match the energetic vocal delivery
- **Special arrangements:** Standard ska-rock arrangement with potential for horn section accents
- **Instrumental breaks:** Solo section (E, A, G#m, C#m, C#) provides space for guitar solos. The bridge section can also be extended for rhythmic variations
- **Key changes:** None - stays in E major throughout
- **Dynamics:** Build energy through verses to chorus, maintain high intensity throughout with aggressive downstrokes during the bridge section
- **Rhythm pattern:** Characteristic ska rhythm with emphasis on off-beats, transitioning to more aggressive punk-style downstrokes in the bridge
