## Performance Notes
- **Style:** Ska fusion with Latin rhythms, featuring characteristic upstroke pattern on off-beats. The song blends ska, reggae, and Latin elements typical of Los de Abajo's sound. The "Latin Ska Force" style combines energetic ska rhythms with Latin musical influences.
- **Bass lines:** The bass provides a solid foundation with root notes on the downbeats and rhythmic fills on the off-beats. The bass line follows the chord progression closely, emphasizing the root of each chord (E, D, C, B, G, A) with occasional passing tones and syncopated patterns that complement the ska rhythm. The bass maintains a driving pulse throughout, especially in the Em-D-Em-C-D pattern that dominates the verses.
- **Special arrangements:** Features collaboration with Julieta Venegas, Cheko Biloba, and Dr. Shenka, adding vocal richness and diverse sonic textures. The arrangement includes dynamic horn section interplay typical of ska music. The song features spoken word sections and energetic vocal delivery.
- **Instrumental breaks:** The bridge section (Em D Em C D pattern repeated) allows for extended solos and instrumental showcases, with space for horn section features and dynamic builds.
- **Key changes:** None - maintains Em throughout the song, with the chorus shifting to Bm-G-A-Bm progression for contrast.
- **Dynamics:** Builds energy from verses to chorus, with emotional intensity peaks in the bridge section. The song contrasts energetic ska rhythms with emotionally charged lyrics about love and loss.
- **Rhythm pattern:** Classic ska rhythm emphasizing beats 2 and 4, with syncopated accents. The bass maintains a steady pulse while the guitar provides the characteristic ska upstroke pattern. The Em-D-Em-C-D pattern creates a driving, danceable groove throughout.
