## Performance Notes
- **Style:** Reggae/ska fusion rhythm with emphasis on off-beats (typical ska upstroke pattern). The song features a laid-back, melodic character with acoustic guitar elements

It emphasizes the "one" beat and utilizes syncopated rhythms common in Latin-influenced rock. The bass provides the rhythmic foundation with melodic lines that follow the chord roots (D, Em, C, G, Bm) while adding rhythmic variations and syncopation
- **Special arrangements:** Standard reggae/ska arrangement with acoustic guitar (guest appearance by Gustavo Santaolalla on original recording) and potential for horn section accents

- **Dynamics:** Maintains a laid-back, upbeat feel throughout with subtle dynamic variations. The chorus provides melodic uplift while maintaining the reggae groove
